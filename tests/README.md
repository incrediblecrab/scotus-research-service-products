# tests

Offline tests: `pip install '.[test]'`, then `python -m pytest -q`. They use no network; the PDF tests need poppler.

**Objective:** pin down the behavior the verbatim record depends on. Each load-bearing check was tested by planting the defect it should catch in a copy of the code (33 defects, last run September 26, 2026): unchanged, the copy passed all 159 tests, and every planted defect failed at least one. **Inputs:** real pages and PDFs in [`fixtures/`](fixtures/README.md), and a stand-in site and listing.

**Files:**

- `conftest.py`: fixture loaders, the stand-in site and listing, and a check that no test leaves a store open.
- `test_sources.py`: each parser on a saved page: counts, and every field in the page's rendered text; for Argument Audio, Year-End Reports, filing pages, news, cited sources and media, and About pages, the file's address, its document type and the typed columns; which collections are asked about on every run; and a term page that shows another term, whose entries are not taken.
- `test_markup.py`: rendered text, whitespace, links.
- `test_extract.py`: raw text unchanged, scanned or born-digital, the pypdf cross-check, and what is taken for MP3 audio and MP4 video.
- `test_http.py`: pacing, robots.txt, redirects, error statuses.
- `test_pipeline.py`: the sync loop: failures and retries, delisting, the emptied-page check and the wrong-term check, `--revalidate-all`, budget and disk stops, the writer lease, listings that mix PDFs, HTML pages, audio and video, and mutable pages read again on every run.
- `test_store.py`: Parquet round trips, an empty partition written without a row group, how a file's columns differ from the schema, both stores' commits and which failures the Hub store retries, and collections sharing a repo under their prefixes.
- `test_verify.py`: each planted data defect is named, including a partition written with other columns, and audio and video whose bytes are no longer audio or video.
- `test_card.py`: front matter, licenses, every gap named, and the category card's configs.
- `test_cli.py`: exit codes, the card check, `--dataset all`, and on Actions: the Trusted Publishing resource per dataset, the reserve per collection still to come, and the step outputs.
- `test_cases.py`: docket keys from every spelling the sources use; the pages of each case's opinion in a file of the United States Reports that holds many, and the pages that end one; a volume of the Reports split into its cases, scanned or born digital, with each case's docket, date, title and citation, and the cases before OT1971; the cases a footnote says were decided together; the addresses tried for each docket format; docket pages found, missing, revalidated with a 304 and refused; the fields and proceedings of new, application, original, old-format and not-found pages; audio links, and none from a listing that shows another term; the recheck rules and term order; runs that leave terms alone until due, budget stops and refusals that keep the stored pages, and the step outputs; and `verify`, including a term file written with other columns.
